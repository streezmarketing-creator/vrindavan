Privacy Policy

Effective: October 2026 · Website Operator: Shiva Chaurasia

1. Website Purpose

This website serves as a property discovery and customer enquiry platform for residential plots, luxury villas, and apartments in Vrindavan, Mathura, Uttar Pradesh. It is maintained to connect prospective buyers with verified project details and representative assistance.

2. Information We Collect

When you complete an enquiry form on our website, we collect:

  • Full Name: To address you accurately during property discussions.
  • Mobile Number: Required to contact you via telephone call or WhatsApp message regarding your enquiry.
  • Preferences: Property type (plot, villa, apartment), purpose (self-use, investment, second home), and budget bracket.
  • Attribution Parameters: Anonymous UTM tags (source, medium, campaign) to analyze advertising effectiveness.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Your contact details are strictly used to reply to your specific enquiry, share verified project details, and arrange site visits upon request. We do not sell, rent, or distribute personal data to third-party telemarketers.

4. Consent & Opt-Out

By checking the consent box or communicating with us on WhatsApp, you grant permission to be contacted regarding properties in Vrindavan. You may revoke consent at any time by messaging “STOP” on WhatsApp or contacting us.
